Non Profit Membership

Benefits include:

  • You'll get a nice plaque with the mayor's stamp on it
  • You get to ride on a float Saint Patty's Day

COST: Free

Bronze Membership

Benefits include:

  • You'll get a bronze plaque with your business name on it.
  • You'll receive a free ticket to the city's annual business fair.

COST: $20

Silver Membership

Benefits include:

  • You'll get a shiny silver plaque with the mayor's stamp on it.
  • You get to attend a private dinner with important business leaders.
  • You'll have your business featured in the city's annual magazine.

COST: $35

Gold Membership

Benefits include:

  • You'll get a golden plaque signed by the mayor.
  • You get to sit in the VIP section during the city's annual parade.
  • You'll be invited to an exclusive dinner with the mayor.

COST: $50
